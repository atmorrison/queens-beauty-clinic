About

A calm, professional space where every treatment is built around your comfort and wellbeing.

Queens Beauty Clinic embodies a commitment to inclusivity, welcoming individuals from all walks of life. Each client can expect a personalised approach, ensuring that their unique beauty journey aligns with their vision of self-love and confidence. With a variety of services, including permanent hair removal options, the clinic provides a supportive environment to enhance what you see in the mirror.

Our mission is to care for and affirm people rather than chase profits. That's why all our profits are reinvested into supporting marginalised communities. Beauty that's both affordable and ethical!
